# Initial Access

## Situation
The SOC is investigating suspicious authentication activity across the bank.

## Investigation objective
Determine which employee account was compromised during the initial access.

## Evidence
Review authentication events in the instructor-provided Splunk environment.

## Walkthrough
/labs/null-national-bank/content/walkthroughs/phase-01.html

## Tasks
1. Identify the compromised employee account.
2. Identify the source IP address for the suspicious login.
3. Identify the workstation targeted during initial access.
4. What Windows event ID records a successful logon?
5. What Windows event ID records a failed logon?
6. Identify the logon type associated with a remote interactive session.
7. Identify the remote access protocol used in the initial access.
8. What authentication technique is indicated by many failed attempts followed by a success?
9. Identify the executable commonly associated with an RDP client.
10. What was the outcome of the attacker's final authentication attempt?
