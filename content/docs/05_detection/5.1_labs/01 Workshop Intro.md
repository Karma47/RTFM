##### Topic: Hypothesis Driven Threat Hunting
**Duration: 4.5 Hours**

**Description:** This Threat Hunting workshop is designed to provide participants with practical experience in identifying, investigating and analysing suspicious activities using a SIEM platform.

Participants will work within a preconfigured environment containing artificially generated security logs that simulate realistic cyber incidents.

Through guided and independent investigation exercises, participants will learn the following:
1. Develop threat hunting hypotheses
2. Analyse security events
3. Correlate logs from multiple sources
4. Identify IOCs and IOAs
5. Reconstruct incident timelines.

The workshop will follow a scenario-based approach, where participants act as Threat Hunters investigating a suspected security incident within a simulated enterprise environment using a Threat Intelligence Report.
